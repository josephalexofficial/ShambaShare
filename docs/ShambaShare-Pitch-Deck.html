ShambaShare

Climate-smart farm tools, closer than you think.

A peer-to-peer marketplace that helps farmers in Eldoret & Uasin Gishu find, request, and share idle climate-smart equipment — online, with clear booking dates and pay on delivery.

The problem

Adaptation tools exist — access does not

Climate pressure

Droughts, unreliable rains, and declining soil health increase the need for irrigation, soil testing, and conservation tools.

High cost of ownership

Solar pumps, soil kits, drip systems, and tillers are expensive for smallholders — yet often sit idle between seasons for owners.

Broken coordination

Informal rentals rely on word of mouth. Farmers struggle to find nearby tools, agree dates, and track requests reliably.

Result: climate-smart equipment is underused while neighboring farms miss planting windows, water crops poorly, and lose income.

The solution

Share idle tools. Build farm resilience.

ShambaShare is a portal-first web marketplace for renting and sharing climate-smart farm equipment locally.

Discover nearby

Browse tools sorted by distance around Eldoret.

Book with dates

Request clear start and return windows online.

Owner earnings

List idle assets and earn when neighbors rent.

Pay on delivery

Practical trust model for early community adoption.

Focus tools
  • Solar irrigation pumps
  • Soil testing kits
  • Drip irrigation kits
  • Conservation tillers
  • Moisture / field tools

Built for smallholders, women & youth farmers, cooperatives, and local equipment owners in Uasin Gishu.

How it works

From listing to rental in four steps

1
Join

Create a Renter, Owner, or Both account with phone contact.

2
List or find

Owners publish tools; renters search nearest available gear.

3
Request

Pick dates, send a booking request, track status in-portal.

4
Pickup & pay

Coordinate handover by phone; pay on delivery; return on date.

One portal for both sides: bookings, listings, rental requests, notifications, and settings — not just a public brochure site.

Market & business

Who pays, and how we grow

Customers

Primary: smallholder renters needing short-term tools.
Secondary: owners & agri-vendors monetizing idle equipment.
Later: cooperatives & climate programs.

Revenue
  • Service fee on completed rentals
  • Featured / priority listings
  • B2B cooperative partnerships

Start light on fees to drive adoption; scale with transaction volume.

Competition

Informal networks, social posts, general classifieds, and dealer hire desks — none combine local climate-tool focus with a renter/owner portal.
