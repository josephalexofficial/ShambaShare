Key Partners

  • Equipment owners & agri-vendors listing idle climate-smart tools
  • Farmer cooperatives & farmer groups for onboarding and trust
  • Agri-extension / climate programs promoting adaptation tool access
  • Local agri-dealers for tool supply, servicing, and referrals
  • EldoHub / Tech for Good ecosystem for mentorship, validation, and visibility
  • County / community networks in Uasin Gishu for farmer reach

Key Activities

  • Operate the web marketplace & member portal
  • Onboard owners and verify listings quality
  • Enable bookings, notifications, and coordination
  • Support renter–owner matching by distance
  • Collect feedback and improve trust flows
  • Grow local inventory of climate-smart tools

Key Resources

  • Product: ShambaShare web MVP & portal
  • Tech stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase-ready backend
  • Local focus: Eldoret / Uasin Gishu market knowledge
  • Network effects: listings + active renters
  • Brand & code: live demo + GitHub repository

Value Propositions

  • For renters: affordable, nearby access to climate-smart tools without buying them outright
  • For owners: earn from idle solar pumps, soil kits, drip kits, and tillers
  • Faster discovery: find tools sorted by distance around Eldoret
  • Clear booking: request with start/return dates and track status in-portal
  • Practical trust: coordinate pickup by phone; pay on delivery
  • Climate resilience: put adaptation equipment to use when weather stress hits
  • One workspace: renter, owner, or both roles in a single portal

Customer Relationships

  • Self-serve join / sign-in and role-based portal
  • In-app notifications for requests & returns
  • Direct phone coordination at pickup
  • Owner listing support & availability controls
  • Community demos and farmer feedback loops
  • Future: cooperative account managers

Channels

  • Web platform: shamba-share-site.vercel.app
  • Mobile-responsive portal (phone-first use)
  • Farmer groups, co-ops, and agri networks
  • Local demos / field validation sessions
  • Hackathon & climate-tech showcases
  • Referrals between owners and renters

Customer Segments

  • Primary: smallholder farmers needing short-term climate-smart tools
  • Women & youth farmers seeking lower-cost adaptation access
  • Equipment owners with idle irrigation, soil, and tillage assets
  • Agri-vendors / service providers listing rentable inventory
  • Farmer cooperatives coordinating member access
  • Later: climate programs & agribusiness partners buying structured access

Cost Structure

  • Product development: engineering, hosting, domain, maintenance
  • Customer acquisition: field demos, onboarding, community outreach
  • Operations: listing quality checks, support, dispute handling
  • Partnerships: cooperative engagement and local activation
  • Future scale: payments integrations, storage, agent network, county expansion

Revenue Streams

  • Marketplace service fee on completed rentals (percentage or flat booking fee)
  • Featured listings for owners wanting higher visibility
  • B2B / cooperative packages for member tool-access programs
  • Later: premium owner tools, institutional climate partnerships, and regional expansion fees
  • Early stage keeps fees light to maximize adoption; revenue scales with transaction volume across Uasin Gishu and nearby counties
